Classifiers¶

Classifiers describe the contents of an image, and are attached to formats with square brackets, e.g. NiftiGz[T1w] or DicomSeries[Mri, Brain]. Formats can be given one classifier from each of the modality, anatomy and derivative groups. Abbreviations (e.g. T1w, Mri) are aliases of the classes listed here.
